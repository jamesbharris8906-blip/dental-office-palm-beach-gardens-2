General Dentistry

General dentistry is the base of good oral health. It covers checkups, cleanings, X-rays, fillings, and gum care. At Steven Weiss DDS, a routine visit starts with a look at your teeth and gums, a check for spots that need care, and a cleaning that leaves your mouth feeling fresh. The team also talks with you about brushing, flossing, and food choices that help your teeth stay strong between visits. Small problems get caught early here, when they are easy and cheap to fix. That is the whole point of coming in twice a year, even when nothing hurts.

Invisalign®

Plenty of adults and teens in Palm Beach Gardens want straighter teeth without metal brackets. Invisalign clear aligners move teeth a little at a time with trays you can take out to eat and brush. Dr. Weiss is a top Invisalign provider, and the team maps out your plan with scans and photos so you can see where your teeth are headed. Most people wear each set of trays for about two weeks, then switch to the next set. You can still eat your favorite foods, and most folks will not even notice you are wearing them. If you have crowded teeth, gaps, or a bite that feels off, ask about a consult.

Dental Implants

When a tooth is gone, an implant can take its place in a way that looks and feels like the real thing. A small titanium post goes into the jaw, and after it heals, a crown goes on top. You can chew, talk, and smile with it just like a natural tooth. Implants also help keep the jawbone strong, which matters when a tooth has been missing for a while. The team handles single implants, implant bridges, and full-mouth work, and they talk through cost, timing, and healing at your first visit so you know what to expect.

Emergency

Teeth do not wait for a good time to break. A cracked tooth, a tooth that got knocked out on the field, or pain that keeps you up at night all count as urgent. Call the office at (561) 260-5575 as soon as you can, and tell the team what happened. They will fit you in as soon as they can and tell you what to do until you get there. If a tooth comes out, keep it moist, do not scrub the root, and bring it with you. Fast care can save a tooth, and it can stop a small crack from turning into a big problem.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is about how your smile looks when you laugh, talk, and meet new people. Whitening, veneers, bonding, and reshaping can fix stains, chips, gaps, and worn edges. Dr. Weiss looks at the shape of your face, the shade of your teeth, and the way you smile before he suggests anything. The goal is a smile that looks like you, only brighter and more even. You do not need a full makeover to see a change. Sometimes one small fix makes the whole smile look new.

Oral Surgery

Some tooth problems need surgery, and the team handles those in the office. That includes pulling teeth that cannot be saved, taking out wisdom teeth that hurt or crowd other teeth, and preparing the mouth for implants. They explain the steps, the healing time, and the aftercare in plain words. You will leave with clear notes on what to eat, how to keep the area clean, and when to call if something feels wrong. Most people are back to normal food and work within a few days.

Teeth Cleanings

A cleaning is more than a polish. The hygienist clears away plaque and tartar that your brush cannot reach, checks your gums for swelling or bleeding, and polishes your teeth smooth. Cleanings also give the team a chance to spot early gum trouble before it turns into something worse. Come in twice a year, and your teeth will thank you for it.

Dental Exams

An exam is a full look at your mouth, teeth, gums, bite, and jaw. The team takes X-rays when needed and checks old fillings and crowns for wear. They also screen for signs of trouble you cannot feel yet. After the exam, they sit with you and go over what they found, what needs care now, and what can wait. You leave with a plan, not a puzzle.

Composite Fillings

A cavity does not have to mean a silver spot in your smile. Tooth-colored composite fillings match the shade of your tooth, so the repair blends in. The team cleans out the decay, fills the space, and shapes it to fit your bite. The visit is quick, and you can eat normally once the numbness wears off.

Dental Crowns and Caps

A crown covers a tooth that is cracked, worn, or weak after a root canal. It works like a cap that guards the tooth and brings back its shape and strength. Crowns are made to match your other teeth, so no one can tell which tooth was fixed. With good care, a crown can last for many years.

Teeth Whitening

Coffee, tea, red wine, and time can all dull a smile. Whitening lifts stains and brings back a brighter shade in one visit or with take-home trays. The team checks your teeth first to make sure whitening is safe for you, then picks the shade that looks natural on you. A brighter smile can change how you feel in photos and at work.

Veneers

Veneers are thin shells that go on the front of teeth to fix chips, gaps, stains, and uneven shapes. They are made to fit your face and your smile, and they can change a smile in just a couple of visits. Dr. Weiss is known for cosmetic work, and veneers are one of the tools he uses most. If you have ever hidden your smile in photos, ask if veneers could help.

Dental Bonding

Bonding is a fast, low-cost way to fix a small chip or gap. The team puts a tooth-colored resin on the tooth, shapes it, and hardens it with a light. Most bonding visits take less than an hour, and you walk out with the fix done. It is a good choice when you want a small change without a big bill.

Dentures

If you have lost several teeth, dentures can bring back your bite and your smile. The team makes full and partial dentures that fit well and look natural. They also work with implants to hold dentures steady, so you can eat and talk without worry. A good fit matters, and the team will adjust your dentures until they feel right.

Root Canal Therapy

A root canal sounds scary, but it is really a way to save a tooth and stop pain. When the soft tissue inside a tooth gets infected, the team cleans it out, seals the tooth, and covers it with a crown. Most patients say it feels like getting a filling. The pain you had before the visit is usually gone once the tooth heals.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom teeth often run out of room. They can push on other teeth, trap food, and cause pain or swelling. The team takes X-rays to see how your wisdom teeth sit, then talks with you about taking them out before they cause more trouble. Healing takes a few days of soft food and rest, and the team gives you clear aftercare notes.

Tooth Extractions

Sometimes a tooth cannot be saved, and taking it out is the kindest choice. The team pulls teeth gently, with care for your comfort, and talks with you about what comes next, whether that is an implant, a bridge, or a partial denture. You will leave with steps for healing and a plan for replacing the tooth.

TMJ Treatments

Jaw pain, clicking, and headaches can come from the jaw joint, called the TMJ. The team checks your bite and jaw movement, then suggests care that may include a night guard, bite work, or simple home steps. Many people feel better once their bite is balanced and the jaw can rest.
